⭐ A Star Named After You ⭐

Out of all the stars in the sky, I decided to dedicate one to you.

Not because you're perfect. Not because you're the brightest.

But because somehow, in a very short amount of time, you became one of my favorite people to talk to.

We've shared random conversations, coffee discussions that probably lasted longer than they should have, terrible jokes, endless teasing, and an impressive number of flirts that somehow flew right over your head.

Honestly, I still don't know whether you're innocent, clueless, or secretly pretending not to notice.

I guess the mystery remains unsolved.

Thank you for being the kind of person who can make an ordinary day feel less ordinary.

Thank you for laughing at stupid things.

Thank you for tolerating my nonsense.

And thank you for being the person who wakes up to random doodles because apparently my brain decides that drawing good morning art at ridiculous hours is a perfectly normal thing to do.

I hope this year brings you more happiness than stress, more coffee than tired mornings, more adventures than excuses, and more reasons to smile than reasons to overthink.

And if life ever gets noisy, just remember:

Somewhere in the universe there's a tiny star with your name on it, created by a friend who thinks you're pretty awesome.

Happy Birthday, Coffee Girl 🐣🧿
